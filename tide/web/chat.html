What am I looking at?

Tide is still a next-word model: it is scored on its guess of the very next word, like GPT-2. But that guess is the front of a belt of drafts. The rightmost draft is a brand-new, blurry thought about the word several words away. As each real word is read, every draft moves one place closer to now and gets one clean-up that weighs what was read so far (its memories) and the drafts behind it (the further future). By the time a thought reaches the front, it has been cleaned once per slot.

Far drafts are naturally unsure (several words ahead is hard to know). Watch how a draft sharpens as it moves left.
