Bring a model onto the phone

  1. 1
    On the pod: python3 -m phone.export --run coral writes a phone copy (8-bit, about 1.2 GB; Bee_v2_0.2B is 0.25 GB).
  2. 2
    python3 -m phone.serve, then open the pod's 8001 link on this phone and tap Open in Oazy.
  3. 3
    Or copy the .oazy file to the phone any way you like and add it here.
