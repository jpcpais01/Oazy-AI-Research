How does Memtrix read?

Memtrix never looks back at the text. Every block holds a few small memories (grids of 64 × 64 numbers), blank when a text starts. For each word, every memory guesses what goes with it, compares with the truth, and is fixed by its surprise. Then it is asked "what comes next?" and answers from the updated memory.

Memory surprise shades each word by how wrong the memories' guesses were (bright = new to them). Word surprise shades by how unlikely the model thought the word was. Click any word to inspect it. The text continues between messages; New text wipes the memories.

What do the colors mean?

Orange numbers are positive, blue negative, dark near zero. Rows are "situations" (keys), columns are "answers" (values). The right picture is the stamp: exactly what this one word added, which is its key times its surprise, scaled by trust. A word the memory already knew leaves a faint stamp.
