Coral

a billion parts, only a few awake at a time

Twenty layers, each with 32 experts. For every word a small router wakes two of them, so only 161M of its 1,037M parameters do the work. After a short chat lesson it runs offline on a phone.

The cost of a model, and what it knows

Each pearl is a model: further right cost more to train, higher picks the sensible ending more often. Ours are what we paid for the GPU. The published models are our estimates from their size and the text they read.

HellaSwag, scored per letter, 10,042 questions. Pearl size follows parameter count; Coral's bright core is the part at work per word.
The training text

Pearl

ordinary text, and small worlds

Mostly carefully chosen web pages, books, Wikipedia, maths and code. One part in five is Field1: little worlds told in plain, varied words, where every answer is known exactly, so a model can learn to keep track of who has what and where things are.
