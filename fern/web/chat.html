What am I looking at?

Fern is not a next-word model. It never writes left to right. For every passage of 16 words it first decides where the passage is going (word 16), then the middle of the way there (word 8), then the middle of each half (4 and 12), then of each quarter, and in the last round it fills every remaining gap. One rule, five times: decide the middle of every stretch whose two ends are known.

So a word in round 5 is chosen knowing the words on both sides of it, and a round-1 word is chosen from the past alone. Everything before the passage is held by Fern's reader, Engram's learn-while-reading memory, so long texts cost the same per word.
