How does Bee read?

Bee is a stack of 24 layers. Three in every four are memory layers (Memtrix): small grids of numbers, fixed by their own surprise word after word, that carry the gist and the order of everything so far. Every fourth is an attention layer: the newest word may look back at any earlier word exactly, which is how Bee can copy a name or a number from far back.

Bee is a next-word model: it writes one word at a time. Near the end of its training it read conversations written as User: … / Assistant: …, so in Chat it answers as the assistant. Write lets it carry on any text. Nothing is re-read between messages: the memories and the attention layers' notes simply carry on.

Say hello, or ask something. Try telling Bee a fact early on ("My cat is called Pepper.") and asking about it later, then click the answer to see which attention layer looked back at "Pepper".
